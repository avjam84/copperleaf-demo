How it works

  1. Customer submits the form (or emails or messages).
  2. Instant, personalised reply confirms the details and next step.
  3. Urgent keywords (leak, flood, gas, no heating) are escalated to a human straight away. Gas smells go straight to the National Gas Emergency number.
  4. After hours: the customer gets a clear auto-response and a callback slot for the morning.
  5. Every lead is logged so nothing slips through.
